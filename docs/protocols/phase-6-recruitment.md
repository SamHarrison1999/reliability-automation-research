# Phase 6 participant recruitment protocol

**Status:** Protocol-ready recruitment design; recruitment prohibited until
ethics approval

## Target population

Adults with professional experience relevant to software-service operation and
incident decision-making.

## Feasibility sample

Target:

- 8 to 12 participants.

This is a feasibility sample.

It is not a statistical-power justification and will not support generalisable
population-effect claims.

## Inclusion criteria

Participants must:

1. be aged 18 or over;
2. be able to provide informed consent;
3. have at least 12 months cumulative professional experience in one or more of:
   - software engineering;
   - DevOps;
   - site reliability engineering;
   - cloud/platform engineering;
   - production support; or
   - another role involving software-service incidents;
4. be able to complete the English-language study materials; and
5. have access to a normal desktop/laptop web browser for the remote session.

Kubernetes expertise is not required.

## Exclusion criteria

Exclude potential participants who:

- are under 18;
- cannot provide informed consent;
- have seen the confidential task answer keys;
- are in a direct dependent or supervisory relationship with the researcher;
- are a close family member of the researcher; or
- cannot safely or reliably access the controlled study interface.

No exclusion is based on disability or health status.

Reasonable adjustments should be considered first.

## Recruitment channels

After approval, recruitment may use:

- professional software-engineering networks;
- relevant professional online communities;
- LinkedIn or equivalent professional networks;
- university/professional contacts where gatekeeper requirements are met; and
- participant referral only where the approved ethics materials permit it.

Recruitment messages must not be posted before the required favourable ethics
opinion and other approvals exist.

## Avoiding coercion

The researcher must not recruit anyone over whom they have educational,
employment, clinical, financial or supervisory authority.

Participation is voluntary.

Recruitment materials must make clear that:

- taking part has no effect on employment, education or professional standing;
- the study is not a technical job interview or competence assessment;
- participants may stop without giving a reason; and
- compensation is for time rather than performance.

## Planned compensation

The application-stage budget assumes a GBP 20 digital voucher per participant
for a session expected to last approximately 60-75 minutes.

Maximum feasibility budget for 12 completed sessions:

GBP 240.

The payment:

- is compensation for time;
- is not performance-dependent;
- is not described as a research benefit; and
- must be confirmed with an approved funding source before recruitment begins.

The final ethics submission and participant documents must state the actual
approved funding source and payment process.

## Recruitment screening

Screening collects only information necessary to establish eligibility:

- confirmation of age 18+;
- broad role category;
- approximate years of relevant professional experience;
- broad incident-response experience; and
- ability to use the remote study format.

Do not request:

- exact date of birth;
- employer/customer names;
- health information;
- ethnicity;
- religion;
- political affiliation;
- sexual orientation; or
- other special-category information.

## Contact-data separation

Recruitment email/contact information is operational personal data.

It must be stored separately from task responses.

The participant observation dataset uses only a study pseudonym.

## Recruitment stop conditions

Recruitment must stop if:

- ethics approval is absent, expired or withdrawn;
- a required amendment has not been approved;
- participant materials no longer match the approved protocol;
- the compensation source is unavailable;
- the task interface has an unresolved safety/data issue; or
- the target feasibility sample has been reached.
