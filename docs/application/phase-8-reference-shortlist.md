# Phase 8 proposal reference shortlist

**Status:** Proposal shortlist, not a completed systematic-review bibliography

## Shahin et al. (2017)

M. Shahin, M. A. Babar, M. Zahedi and L. Zhu.

*Beyond Continuous Delivery: An Empirical Investigation of Continuous
Deployment Challenges.*

ESEM 2017.

DOI: `10.1109/ESEM.2017.18`

Use:

- empirical continuous-deployment context;
- practitioner/organisational constraints;
- motivation for studying automation as more than a purely technical process.

## Shahin et al. (2019)

M. Shahin, M. Zahedi, M. A. Babar and L. Zhu.

*An Empirical Study of Architecting for Continuous Delivery and Deployment.*

Empirical Software Engineering, 24, 1061-1108.

DOI: `10.1007/s10664-018-9651-4`

Use:

- software architecture and continuous-delivery context;
- operations concerns;
- independently deployable units;
- justification for explicitly defining software architecture.

## Chen et al. (2020)

J. Chen et al.

*How Incidental are the Incidents? Characterizing and Prioritizing Incidents
for Large-Scale Online Service Systems.*

ASE 2020.

Use:

- real online-service incident context;
- incident prioritisation and operational evidence;
- distinction between system incidents and direct human-measurement evidence.

## Meiklejohn et al. (2021)

C. Meiklejohn et al.

*Service-Level Fault Injection Testing.*

SoCC 2021.

Use:

- controlled service-level failures;
- microservice resilience testing;
- methodological relevance of systematic fault injection.
