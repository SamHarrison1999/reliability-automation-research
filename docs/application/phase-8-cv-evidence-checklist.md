# Phase 8 CV/application evidence checklist

## Academic preparation

Include accurately:

- BSc degree and classification;
- MSc Computer Science and classification;
- dissertation topic/result where useful;
- research-methods preparation where useful.

## Professional relevance

Highlight genuinely supported experience in:

- professional software development;
- Java;
- frontend/web development;
- CI/CD;
- Jenkins/GitHub Actions;
- Docker;
- Kubernetes;
- release management; and
- reliability/operational work where directly supported.

## Research preparation

Include a concise entry for:

`reliability-automation-research`

Describe:

- reproducible OrderFlow benchmark;
- controlled fault experiments;
- versioned empirical protocol;
- preserved technical evidence;
- deterministic analysis; and
- human-participant protocol preparation.

Do not describe the Phase 5 technical pilot as a publication or as an
established general scientific result.

## Cross-document consistency

The CV, covering letter, supervisor message and proposal should use the same:

- project title;
- two-RQ framing;
- technical-pilot status;
- participant-study status;
- ethics status; and
- evidence boundary.

## External application materials

The repository does not substitute for:

- the final CV;
- degree certificates;
- transcripts;
- referee details;
- the then-current application form/portal; or
- any other document required by the OU at submission time.
