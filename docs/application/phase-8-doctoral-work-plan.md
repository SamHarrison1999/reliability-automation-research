# Phase 8 provisional doctoral work plan

This plan is milestone-based rather than tied to an unverified full-time or
part-time registration mode.

## Work package 1 ? literature and protocol refinement

- complete systematic/scoping review execution;
- refine contribution/gap statement;
- review benchmark external validity;
- refine technical experimental protocol;
- update participant protocol with supervisors;
- submit required ethics/data-protection material.

## Work package 2 ? technical study expansion

- replicate/extend controlled technical experiments;
- justify additional fault/system conditions;
- pre-specify analysis;
- preserve provenance and exclusions;
- evaluate transferability beyond the initial OrderFlow slice where feasible.

## Work package 3 ? human feasibility and main study

After required approval:

- execute participant feasibility;
- revise tasks/instruments where justified;
- justify main-study sample;
- freeze main participant analysis;
- recruit/collect according to approved protocol.

## Work package 4 ? socio-technical integration

- analyse technical and human streams separately;
- integrate at interpretation level;
- identify convergent/divergent automation effects;
- document validity and scope conditions.

## Work package 5 ? dissemination and thesis

- peer-reviewed technical/method paper where justified;
- human/software-engineering empirical paper where justified;
- integrated thesis chapters;
- reproducibility/data-access package;
- final limitations and implications.

## Dependencies

Participant work depends on ethics/institutional approval.

Access to professional participants is a feasibility risk.

Additional software domains depend on available time/resources and must not
make the doctorate unmanageably broad.
