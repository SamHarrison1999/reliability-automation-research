# Draft PhD application covering letter

Dear Research Degrees Admissions Team,

I am applying for doctoral research within the Open University School of
Computing and Communications with the proposed project **Reliability Automation
and Software Engineering Practice in Continuous Delivery Environments**.

The project investigates reliability automation as a socio-technical software
engineering problem. It combines controlled experiments on a reproducible
cloud-native transactional benchmark with a separately ethics-reviewed
participant study of software engineers' diagnosis, recovery and escalation
decisions.

I hold a First-Class BSc and a Distinction MSc in Computer Science. My
professional experience includes software engineering and release-management
work involving Java, web technologies, CI/CD, Jenkins/GitHub Actions, Docker
and Kubernetes.

I have also undertaken substantial pre-application research preparation. I
have developed the bounded OrderFlow experimental system, validated
reproducible Kubernetes execution, completed a 50-observation technical pilot,
and designed the separate human-participant protocol including recruitment,
measurement, consent and data-management boundaries.

I regard this work as evidence of methodological feasibility rather than as
completed doctoral research.

The proposal has been tightened following earlier feedback from Dr Christopher
Douce. The revised design now uses two connected research questions, explicitly
defines the software studied and its construction, identifies the sources of
technical and participant evidence, and states the principal validity and
ethics limitations.

I believe the project fits the School's strengths in empirical software
engineering and automated/adaptive software systems while contributing a
direct software-engineering-practice dimension.

Thank you for considering my application.

Yours faithfully,

Samuel Harrison
