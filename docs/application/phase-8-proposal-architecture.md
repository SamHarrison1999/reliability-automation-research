# Phase 8 OU proposal architecture

## Constraint

Target proposal length:

600-1000 words.

References are retained separately from the main narrative when measuring the
working word budget.

## Planned structure

### Title

One concise sentence identifying:

- reliability automation;
- software-engineering practice; and
- continuous-delivery context.

### Background ? target approximately 200-250 words

Must establish:

- continuous-delivery/reliability context;
- automation as a socio-technical intervention;
- relevant empirical literature;
- preliminary rather than falsely systematic gap;
- why the question matters.

### Aim and Method ? target approximately 400-500 words

Must contain:

- one overall aim;
- exactly two principal RQs;
- explicit OrderFlow definition;
- construction/selection rationale;
- technical evidence sources;
- human evidence sources;
- distinction between Phase 5 pilot and future doctoral evidence;
- ethics boundary;
- analysis/integration approach;
- validity limitations.

### Relevance ? target approximately 100-150 words

Must explain:

- academic contribution;
- practical relevance;
- likely beneficiaries;
- fit with software-engineering research.

### Your suitability ? target approximately 100-150 words

Must connect:

- qualifications;
- professional software-engineering experience;
- CI/CD and cloud-native experience;
- existing pilot preparation;
- ability to execute the proposed methodology.

## Word-budget objective

Preferred main-text range:

850-950 words.

This gives enough detail to answer earlier methodological criticism without
approaching the 1000-word ceiling too closely.

## Proposal writing rule

The proposal should focus on the proposed doctorate.

Repository detail is used only when it demonstrates feasibility or resolves a
methodological ambiguity.

The application should not read like a software-project README.
