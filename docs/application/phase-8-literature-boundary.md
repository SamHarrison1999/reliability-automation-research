# Phase 8 literature boundary

**Status:** Application-level literature boundary; full Phase 1 review remains open

## Existing evidence state

The repository currently contains a nine-record seed evidence matrix and a
provisional literature map.

The named database searches, complete screening, full-text charting and final
synthesis required by the Phase 1 scoping-review protocol have not been
completed.

Phase 8 must therefore not describe the systematic/scoping review as complete.

## What Phase 8 may use

The proposal may use carefully selected, individually verified literature to:

- establish continuous-delivery and architecture context;
- motivate controlled incident/reliability research;
- demonstrate that incident-response automation is an empirical
  software-engineering problem;
- justify fault-injection methodology; and
- motivate direct human evidence.

Where the repository's current map supports only a preliminary gap, the
proposal must describe it as preliminary.

## What Phase 8 may not say

The proposal must not claim that:

- a systematic review has proven the literature gap;
- no previous research exists;
- all relevant databases have been searched;
- all eligible studies have been screened; or
- the seed matrix is representative of the entire field.

## Selected application literature

The current proposal shortlist draws from the existing evidence matrix:

- Shahin et al. (2017), continuous-deployment challenges;
- Shahin et al. (2019), architecture for continuous delivery/deployment;
- Chen et al. (2020), large-scale online-service incidents;
- Meiklejohn et al. (2021), service-level fault-injection testing.

Additional references may be added only after their bibliographic details and
relevance have been checked.

## Carry-forward

The complete Phase 1 review remains valuable work and should be completed
during later application hardening or early doctoral work.

Its incompleteness is a limitation, not a reason to manufacture review
completion during Phase 8.
