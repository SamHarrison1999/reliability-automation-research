# Phase 8 Open University application requirements

**Verification date:** 2026-08-12

**Status:** Current requirements snapshot; re-verification required immediately before submission

## School-specific proposal requirements

The Open University School of Computing and Communications currently asks
applicants preparing their own research proposal to provide a proposal of
approximately 600-1000 words.

The School's proposal guidance asks the applicant to structure the proposal
around:

- Title;
- Background;
- Aim and Method;
- Relevance; and
- Your suitability.

The proposal should:

- be clear and concise;
- define measurable objectives;
- explain the proposed research method;
- explain what evidence would answer the research questions;
- establish academic/practical relevance; and
- explain the expertise the applicant brings.

## Application package

The School currently identifies application materials including:

- research proposal;
- covering letter;
- CV;
- the applicable application information/form;
- referee details;
- relevant degree certificates/transcripts; and
- English-language evidence where applicable.

The exact application route must be re-verified before submission because the
OU is transitioning its research-degree application process for future intakes.

This repository therefore does not hard-code a submission email, form or
deadline as permanently valid.

## Supervisor engagement

Current School guidance strongly recommends discussing a possible proposal with
potential supervisors before formal application.

The initial contact should be concise and should include:

- a CV; and
- a brief description of the intended research.

Supervisor interest, availability and supervisory-team composition are external
decisions and are not implied by this repository.
