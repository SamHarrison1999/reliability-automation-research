# Draft supervisor follow-up email

**Subject:** Revised PhD proposal - reliability automation and software engineering practice

Dear Dr Douce,

Thank you again for the feedback you gave me on my earlier PhD proposal.

I have now substantially revised the project in response to your comments. In
particular, I have reduced the proposal to two connected research questions,
defined the software being studied as a purpose-built transactional benchmark
called OrderFlow, documented why that software was selected, and made the
technical and human empirical-data sources explicit.

I have also completed a bounded technical feasibility and pilot programme. The
prototype can be deployed and reset reproducibly, and the frozen technical
pilot resolved all 50 planned slots from 53 preserved attempts. I am treating
those results as feasibility and descriptive evidence rather than as proof
that automation is generally superior.

For the human/software-engineering side, I have designed a separate
participant-study protocol focused on engineers' diagnosis, recovery and
escalation decisions. No participant data have been collected and formal
ethics approval would still be required before that work could begin.

I would be very grateful for any comments you have on the revised proposal.
If you still feel that the topic may align more closely with colleagues
elsewhere in the School, I would also appreciate any suggestions or circulation
to colleagues whose expertise may fit the technical or socio-technical aspects
of the project.
