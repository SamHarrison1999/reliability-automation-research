# Phase 6 participant debrief - protocol draft

**Status:** Draft for future ethics review

## Thank you

Thank you for contributing to the proposed study.

The study investigates how engineers diagnose and respond to structured
software-service incidents under different decision-support conditions.

## Automation recommendations

The automation-assisted tasks were deliberately designed so that automation was
not always correct.

You were informed before the study that this could occur.

The purpose is to observe whether participants appropriately accept, override
or escalate recommendations rather than to measure unconditional compliance.

## Task design

The incidents were synthetic OrderFlow scenarios.

No production system was affected.

Some tasks also restricted the participant's simulated authority. In those
tasks escalation could be the appropriate action even when the underlying
technical fault was identifiable.

## Interpretation

The feasibility study is intended to evaluate the protocol and measurements.

Individual performance is not a professional assessment.

## Withdrawal

The approved debrief will repeat the withdrawal deadline and the approved
researcher contact route.

The current protocol plans a 14-calendar-day withdrawal window following the
session.

## Questions or concerns

The approved institutional version will contain researcher, supervisor and
ethics contact routes.

This repository draft must not be used as a substitute for those approved
contacts.
