# Phase 6 withdrawal procedure

**Status:** Planned procedure for ethics review

## During a session

A participant may stop immediately.

No reason is required.

The researcher records only the minimum operational information required to
apply the participant's withdrawal choice.

## After a session

The proposed withdrawal deadline is 14 calendar days after participation.

During that period the participant can request withdrawal through the contact
route in the approved Participant Information Sheet.

The researcher uses the separately stored identity-to-study-ID mapping to
identify the relevant research records.

## Withdrawal action

Where withdrawal is requested within the approved window:

- remove task/questionnaire research records for that participant from the
  active research dataset;
- record a non-identifying withdrawal/audit event if required for governance;
- retain or destroy consent/administrative records according to the approved
  institutional requirements; and
- do not replace missing participant data through imputation.

## After anonymisation

Once the identity link has been destroyed and the dataset has crossed the
declared anonymisation boundary, individual data may no longer be identifiable
for removal.

The Participant Information Sheet must communicate this clearly.

## Public outputs

A withdrawal request cannot normally remove information that has already been
irreversibly anonymised and incorporated into a published aggregate output.

The approved participant materials must describe the final applicable rule.
