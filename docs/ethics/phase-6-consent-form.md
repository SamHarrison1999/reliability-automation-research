# Participant consent form - protocol draft

**Status:** Draft for future ethics submission

**Do not use to enrol participants.**

Before approved use, the current institutional consent template, ethics
reference and approved researcher/supervisor contact details must be applied.

A future participant will be asked to confirm each statement.

- I have read and understood the approved Participant Information Sheet.
- I have had the opportunity to ask questions.
- I understand that participation is voluntary.
- I understand that I may stop the session without giving a reason.
- I understand the stated withdrawal deadline and anonymisation boundary.
- I understand that the study uses simulated software incidents rather than
  production systems.
- I understand that automation recommendations may be incorrect.
- I understand what research data will be collected.
- I understand that recruitment/contact information is stored separately from
  task responses.
- I understand how my confidentiality will be protected.
- I understand the proposed retention and research-data-management approach.
- I understand whether appropriately anonymised data may be reused/shared as
  described in the approved information sheet.
- I agree to take part in the study.

Consent evidence will be retained according to the approved institutional
retention schedule and separately from task observations.
