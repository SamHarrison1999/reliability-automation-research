# Phase 6 closeout

## Status

**Application-preparation state:** protocol-ready.

**Ethics approval:** not obtained.

**Participant recruitment:** prohibited.

**Participant data collection:** prohibited.

## Objective

Phase 6 designed an ethics-ready human-participant protocol for RQ2 while
keeping Phase 5 technical evidence separate.

## Completed package

Phase 6 now contains:

- human-study design boundary;
- human measurement strategy;
- NASA-TLX/raw workload plan;
- behavioural reliance-calibration plan;
- recruitment and eligibility protocol;
- accessibility/risk plan;
- four matched OrderFlow task variants;
- deterministic counterbalancing;
- scoring rubrics;
- descriptive feasibility analysis plan;
- Participant Information Sheet draft;
- consent form draft;
- debrief draft;
- withdrawal procedure;
- ethics risk assessment;
- draft Data Management Plan;
- data-protection plan;
- pseudonymisation boundary; and
- automated synthetic protocol checks.

## Human study design

The planned feasibility study is:

- within-participant;
- counterbalanced;
- 8-12 adult software/DevOps/SRE/platform/production-support practitioners;
- four synthetic incident tasks;
- two H-M tasks;
- two H-A tasks;
- one correct and one incorrect automation recommendation in H-A; and
- descriptive feasibility analysis only.

## Evidence boundary

No participant observation exists.

No Phase 5 technical observation is converted into participant evidence.

The participant study is not the same intervention as the Phase 5 autonomous
technical controller.

## Institutional boundary

Current OU guidance requires appropriate ethics review before participant data
collection by OU postgraduate researchers.

The project is currently at application-preparation stage.

Therefore Phase 6 can be declared protocol-ready, but cannot be declared
ethics-approved.

Formal ethics, DMP/Data Protection and any other institutional requirements
must be completed through the process applicable after registration and before
participant recruitment.

## Phase 7

Phase 7 remains deferred/blocked.

It may begin only after the required approvals exist.

No informal friends/family feasibility participants are permitted as a
workaround.

## Phase 8

Phase 8 proposal/application preparation may proceed while Phase 7 is blocked,
provided the proposal accurately states that:

- the technical pilot has been completed;
- the participant protocol is designed;
- participant feasibility has not yet been executed; and
- future human-data collection is conditional on ethics approval.

This is an explicit application-preparation pathway, not a claim that Phase 7
has been completed.

## Phase 6 decision

The Phase 6 application-preparation exit criterion is satisfied:

> The human-participant protocol, recruitment, tasks, consent, data handling and
> analysis plan are complete and internally validated for future institutional
> review.

Formal favourable ethics opinion remains an external execution gate.
