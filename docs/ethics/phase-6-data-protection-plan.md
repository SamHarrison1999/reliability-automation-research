# Phase 6 data-protection plan

**Status:** Draft for future institutional review

## Personal data expected

Even with data minimisation, the project will process personal data during:

- recruitment;
- scheduling;
- consent;
- compensation administration; and
- the temporary pseudonym-to-identity mapping.

Pseudonymised research data can remain personal data while the re-identification
key exists.

## Special-category data

The study does not intentionally request special-category data.

Participants are instructed not to disclose unnecessary personal or
confidential information in typed explanations.

Incidental identifiers are removed/replaced during the approved
de-identification process while retaining an audit trail appropriate to the
institutional policy.

## Separation

Store separately:

### Identity/administration store

- name/contact route;
- scheduling;
- consent reference;
- compensation administration;
- participant-to-study-ID mapping.

### Research store

- study participant ID;
- task responses;
- timings;
- workload ratings;
- recommendation responses;
- explanations;
- derived scores.

## Access

Access is limited to approved members of the research team who require it.

The public GitHub repository has no access to participant data.

## Data Protection review

Current OU guidance indicates that projects processing personal data for
research are likely to require engagement with the Data Protection process,
including cases where personal data will later be anonymised.

The required institutional process must be completed before participant-data
processing begins.

## Rights and transparency

The approved Participant Information Sheet must describe:

- what data are collected;
- purpose;
- storage;
- access;
- retention;
- withdrawal;
- sharing;
- anonymisation; and
- relevant contact/rights routes.

The final lawful-basis and institutional privacy wording must come from the
approved OU Data Protection process rather than being invented in this public
planning repository.

## Data breach/incident handling

Any suspected loss, unauthorised disclosure or data-security incident must be
handled through the then-current institutional information-security/data
protection incident process.

Do not create an informal project-specific substitute for mandatory
institutional reporting.
