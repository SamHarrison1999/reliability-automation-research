# Phase 6 accessibility and risk plan

**Status:** Draft for future institutional ethics review

## Study risk profile

The study uses simulated software-service incidents.

Participants do not interact with production systems, customer data, real
payments or unrestricted infrastructure.

The primary foreseeable burdens are:

- time;
- ordinary task frustration;
- uncertainty when diagnosing an incident;
- possible concern about perceived technical performance; and
- fatigue from repeated tasks/questionnaires.

The study is explicitly not an examination of professional competence.

## Risk controls

- Use a controlled browser interface rather than unrestricted shell access.
- Use synthetic OrderFlow evidence only.
- Do not expose secrets, real infrastructure credentials or customer data.
- Explain that some automation recommendations may be wrong.
- Allow participants to stop at any time.
- Schedule breaks between timed tasks.
- Keep the feasibility session to approximately 60-75 minutes.
- Do not use webcam, audio or screen recording in the initial protocol.
- Do not collect unnecessary sensitive demographic information.
- Do not provide comparative performance rankings to participants.

## Accessibility

The participant interface and documents should support:

- keyboard-only navigation;
- logical focus order;
- semantic headings and controls;
- readable text with browser zoom;
- sufficient contrast;
- no reliance on colour alone;
- plain-language instructions;
- captions/text alternatives for any future media;
- participant-controlled breaks between tasks; and
- remote participation without webcam use.

Reasonable adjustments should be considered individually where they do not
change the construct being measured.

If an adjustment affects a timing measure, retain the task decision evidence
and classify timing according to the pre-specified missing/interpretability
rule rather than excluding the participant.

## Timed-task protection

Normal breaks occur between tasks.

A participant may nevertheless request a pause or stop at any point.

Human welfare takes priority over measurement completeness.

An interrupted task must not have its decision time silently treated as a
normal uninterrupted timing observation.

## Researcher safety

The default protocol is remote.

No lone in-person meeting is required.

If a later amendment introduces in-person sessions, the relevant institutional
lone-working and venue procedures must be reviewed before implementation.

## Privacy minimisation

No audio, video, screen capture, webcam image or biometric data is collected.

Free-text explanations should instruct participants not to include:

- employer/customer names;
- confidential system information;
- personal names; or
- secrets/credentials.

Incidental identifiers in free text must be handled under the approved
de-identification procedure.

## Distress and withdrawal

The tasks are designed to resemble normal professional reasoning rather than
emergency simulation.

Participants may skip a questionnaire item where the approved instrument
allows it and may withdraw from the study.

If a participant becomes uncomfortable, the session ends without pressure to
continue.

## Ethics execution boundary

This plan does not constitute ethics approval.

No participant may be approached or enrolled until the required institutional
review has produced a favourable opinion and all additional required approvals
are in place.
