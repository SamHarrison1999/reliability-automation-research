# Phase 6 ethics risk assessment

**Status:** Draft for institutional review

## Participants

Planned participants are consenting adults with relevant professional
experience.

Children, people unable to consent and dependent groups are not intentionally
recruited.

## Topics

The study concerns professional software-service incident decisions.

It does not intentionally solicit:

- health information;
- trauma;
- political opinions;
- religion;
- ethnicity;
- sexuality;
- criminal behaviour; or
- other special-category information.

## Deception

No deception is planned.

Participants are told before beginning that an automation recommendation may be
incorrect.

Exact task answer keys are not disclosed before task completion because doing
so would invalidate the task, but withholding an answer key is not represented
as false information about the study.

## Technical risk

All incidents/actions are synthetic or simulated.

No participant receives production credentials or unrestricted infrastructure
access.

## Psychological/social risk

Potential risk is limited to ordinary task frustration or concern about
technical performance.

Mitigations include:

- explicit statement that this is not a competence assessment;
- no ranking of participants;
- voluntary withdrawal;
- breaks;
- bounded session length; and
- debriefing.

## Financial risk

Compensation is for time and must be approved as reasonable rather than used as
an inducement.

## Privacy

Data minimisation is used.

No audio/video/screen recording is planned.

Contact/consent information is separated from research responses.

## Researcher risk

Default sessions are remote.

No hazardous fieldwork is required.

## Residual ethics gate

The study cannot begin merely because this assessment exists.

Formal ethics and any required data-protection approvals remain mandatory
execution gates.
