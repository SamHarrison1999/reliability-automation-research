# Phase 8 research-design record: proposal and application preparation

**Project:** PhD Preparation ? Reliability Automation and Software Engineering Practice

**Phase:** 8

**Status:** Proposal/application preparation in progress

**Date started:** 2026-08-12

## Objective

Convert the completed technical preparation and human-study design into a
clear, bounded and evidence-traceable Open University PhD application package.

## Final application research questions

### RQ1 ? technical reliability

Under controlled fault scenarios in a reproducible transactional service
system, how do different recovery-automation strategies affect recovery time,
failure impact and required intervention?

Detection latency remains a shared-detector fidelity/balance measure rather
than being interpreted automatically as a recovery-strategy effect.

### RQ2 ? software-engineering practice

How does automation-assisted incident response influence software engineers'
diagnosis, recovery and escalation decisions during structured
continuous-delivery tasks?

## Working aim

Investigate reliability automation as a socio-technical intervention by
combining reproducible technical experiments with separately collected,
ethics-reviewed evidence of software-engineering decisions.

## Methodological objective

Develop and use a reproducible experimental environment that keeps:

- technical telemetry;
- automation decisions;
- participant decisions;
- participant subjective measures; and
- research interpretation

as distinct evidence classes.

## Empirical object

The application identifies the empirical software explicitly as OrderFlow.

The initial bounded slice contains:

- `order-api`;
- `inventory`;
- synthetic order workload;
- Docker/Kubernetes deployment;
- controlled service-termination and dependency-latency faults; and
- versioned manual/automated recovery paths.

OrderFlow is purpose-built.

Its selection is justified by experimental control, independent service
boundaries, observable dependency failures, reproducible reset and sufficient
complexity for a controlled reliability study.

Its limited realism remains a declared external-validity threat.

## Evidence streams

### RQ1

- structured technical events;
