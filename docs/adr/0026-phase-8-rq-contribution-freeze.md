# ADR 0026: Phase 8 research-question and contribution freeze

- **Status:** Accepted for application drafting
- **Date:** 2026-08-12

## Decision

The formal application will use two primary research questions.

RQ1 concerns controlled technical reliability outcomes.

RQ2 concerns direct engineer diagnosis, recovery and escalation decisions.

Reproducibility is a methodological contribution rather than a third empirical
research question.

## Rationale

This structure responds directly to earlier feedback that the proposal was too
broad and that empirical data sources were insufficiently clear.

It also prevents the technical and human evidence streams from being silently
combined.

## Amendment rule

The application questions may change after supervisor feedback.

Any substantive change before final submission should be recorded rather than
silently overwriting the rationale captured here.
