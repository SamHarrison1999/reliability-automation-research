# ADR 0023: Phase 6 human feasibility analysis boundary

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-12

## Decision

Phase 7 participant work, if approved, is a feasibility study.

The planned 8-12 participant sample will be analysed descriptively.

No p-values, NHST or statistical-significance claims are authorised.

Missing observations are not imputed.

Timing interruptions may invalidate a timing value without automatically
invalidating the participant's decision response.

## Rationale

The initial participant sample is intended to validate protocol operation,
measurement and task realism.

Treating it as a confirmatory effect study would exceed the sample and design.

## Future work

A later confirmatory study requires:

- a frozen revised protocol;
- justified effect/precision target;
- sample-size justification;
- declared inferential model;
- multiplicity strategy where relevant; and
- any required ethics amendment/approval.
