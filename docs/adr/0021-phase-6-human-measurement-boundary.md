# ADR 0021: Phase 6 human measurement boundary

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-12

## Context

RQ2 requires direct evidence of engineer decisions.

The study also has secondary interest in workload, understanding and reliance
on automation, but adding multiple broad questionnaires would increase burden
and risk an unfocused feasibility study.

## Decision

The human study will prioritise:

1. diagnosis accuracy;
2. recovery-decision appropriateness; and
3. escalation appropriateness.

NASA-TLX dimensions will provide the workload measure.

The feasibility protocol will report an explicitly raw/unweighted TLX mean in
addition to the individual dimensions.

Automation reliance will primarily be measured behaviourally through
accept/override/escalate decisions under correct and incorrect
recommendations.

Task-specific confidence/reliance ratings are exploratory and will not be
combined into a general trust scale.

Automation understanding will use four scenario-relevant comprehension items.

## Consequences

The protocol remains closely aligned to RQ2 and avoids treating a questionnaire
score as a substitute for behaviour.

Trust-related claims must remain narrow because the study does not adopt a
general-purpose validated trust questionnaire as a primary instrument.

Future confirmatory work may adopt an additional validated instrument only
through a pre-collection protocol amendment with licensing and psychometric
justification.
